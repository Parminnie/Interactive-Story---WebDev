Good Better Ending

You will take your shot at leaving this damned placed.

You hurry over to the raft, and try to figure out how to use it. It took a few tries to NOT fall off of it after 30 seconds, but hey, you did it.

Wait..how are you supposed to move it? Uhh...

You jump off to quickly find a stick nearby. Luckily, you found a large, sturdy one after only a few minutes of searching.

Alright...now... to the left, then the right, the left..

Woah, look at you, your a pro at this! Nobody could have ever guessed you had dementia, honestly. K, maybe they could've, but just a little.

The creaking raft joined with the sunset's glowing light made to be a truly beautiful scenery. It was only a matter of time before you had reached the other side.

An hour or so passes, and you finally reach the other side. You let go of the stick and get ready to search, and search hard.

And...Eureka! You hear talking! Though you don't exactly see who or where it is. But hey, you have ears. Use them.

And so you do. You step onto the concrete ground and follow the sound. It gets louder and louder, and you finally see it. Civilization, at last.

K and so you talk to them, they help you, yada yada yada, you get saved, so fun, yippee happy ending.

But whether your memory loss is temporary or permanent...well, you'll just have to wait and see.
