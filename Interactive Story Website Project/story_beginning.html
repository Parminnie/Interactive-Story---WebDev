The Forest

It was a morning like none other. You wake up to the sound of the chirping of the birds, singing their morning tune. The sun has just risen. The sky, blue with a tint of yellow near the horizon shined on your eyes, forcing you awake. You... have no memory of who... or where... you are.

You gaze around and see that...you're in the middle of nowhere! Just trees, and grass, and trees, and grass, and... did I mention trees?

Suddenly, you hear a rumble.

Was that a bear?

Scared, you start getting up. Sure, you have zero idea where in the world you are, but it's not like you can just...die?

You hurry near a bush to try to hide, but you hear the rumbling again. Wait, no--it's more like you feel the rumbling noise..?

...Oh.

It seems... your stomach is quite hungry. False alarm, false alarm. No bear here, folks. Just a really hungry man stranded in the middle of nowhere with no knowledge of cooking, fishing, gathering, nothing.

But there's no time like the present to learn! You have two options:

Go search nearby for some fruit, vegetables, heck, maybe even eat grass if you're desperate,

OR

try to look for a body of water to fish for the first time! With... your bare hands..!
