Fish..?

You will do what you CAME here to do. Get your food, and figure the rest out later.

It's a good thing you're wearing shorts and not full on pants. They're ragged, but they'll do. You walk forwards, pull up your sleeves, and get ready to fish.

The cold water gets up to your knees. As you look below, you notice the poor little fishies.

Were you really gonna do this..?

The thought fills you with dread.

Oh, whatever! Just get it over with... you reach out your hand to grab the fish and- woah woah WOAH--

WHAT THE-?

The small fish suddenly turn into... what?! Mermai- no, merMANS???? Oh HELLLLL NAHHHHHHHH--WHAT IS GOING ON???

Now, instead of YOU grabbing THEM, THEY'RE the ones trying to grab YOU... and pull you towards the deep water, UNDERWATER! What do you do?! It's not like you know how to swim?!

Is this really the end...?!

You submerge under the water, and as you do, a thought crosses your mind.

I guess...one should not mess with the little fishies...these litte fishies...
